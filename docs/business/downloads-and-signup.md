# Downloads, optional signup, owner controls

Public free downloads from a **US-based LLC**. No assumption that the company is German-based. No signup provider or email collection is connected in 0.2.0.

## Downloads now

The [README](../../README.md) has six versioned ZIP links with installation instructions. No account/email/key gate. GitHub's source ZIP is not presented as the plugin.

Maintainers control official releases, repository permissions, presentation, issues, and future services. Recipients can fork/redistribute within the license. A public product cannot identify every downloader or installation.

Release download counts are activity, not unique users. Stars/downloads/installs/GitHub notification settings are not a marketing list.

## Optional signup later

A separate invitation may offer release news and practical case-study tips. Keep downloads visible if signup fails. Identify sender, purpose, expected messages, privacy notice, and unsubscribe path. Collect only necessary data; never automatically send a WordPress admin's email/site details.

Keep subscriber data in a private access-controlled service, not GitHub files/issues or shipped browser code. Owner must select/approve a real service before connecting a live form. No pretend signup form is shipped.

This is a product direction, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice or a compliance guarantee. Evaluate actual recipients, services, and practices when implementing the list.

Free local use must remain independent of vendor accounts. Optional support/setup/hosting services can have separate terms without revoking already granted software permissions. [Licensing](licensing.md).
