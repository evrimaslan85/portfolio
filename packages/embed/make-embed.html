Put your portfolio on another website

First upload the included hosted-portfolio folder to your own HTTPS hosting. Then enter the full address of embed.html below. The ZIP itself is not installed in Webflow, Wix, or Squarespace.

For Wix, you can also use Embed Site and paste the hosted HTTPS address directly. Size the frame in the platform's editor. For Webflow and Squarespace, your plan must support the required custom code or iframe block. Test the published page on mobile.

If the host blocks framing with X-Frame-Options or Content-Security-Policy, allow this portfolio page to be framed by your actual site. Change only the intended page's hosting rules. Automatic resizing and editing through the platform's own CMS are not included.
